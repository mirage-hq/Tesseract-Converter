## Brightness/Contrast animation: fail-before, verified render repair

The original native source `brightness_contrast_controls.aep` (14) and direct rendering of the explicit edited FX both change color at 0/0.5/1 seconds. The original fresh AEP has editable keys 0s brightness20/contrast−25 and 1s brightness50/contrast25 (Adobe scripted readback, maximum deviation 0), yet its Adobe render center pixel is `(62,116,166)` at frames 0, 15, 30, 59. Independent native reference centers are `(62,116,166)`, `(62,126,185)`, `(61,141,201)`, `(61,141,201)`. A 0.991 **whole-frame** minimum score therefore did not detect the frozen feature. Resaving the original AEP in Adobe did not fix it; emptying the effect instance's `parT` did not fix it.

A controlled disposable-AEP probe changed only both scalar property descriptors from generic mode8/subtype9 to the mode4/subtype6/flags/`tdsb` records independently observed in the Adobe-authored source. Adobe accepted and rendered it without warning; the four center colors then matched the independent source exactly and all 60 compared frames scored **min=mean=1.0**. The regression `writer::effects::tests::brightness_contrast_animated_scalar_uses_native_effect_record` failed before and passed after changing the converter-internal Brightness & Contrast 2 scalar encoding. The **new writer's fresh output is byte-for-byte identical** to that successfully Adobe-rendered diagnostic AEP: SHA-256 `6f206ecefc03f148303435fafd71119b27c647ec7c84b0e77e224b5773a73a7f`. This proves the fixed **bytes** render correctly in Adobe, not a new control readback: the fresh fixed-file readback remains **unrun** because another workspace took ownership of the Adobe GUI session.
