# Community conduct

Keep discussions respectful, specific and focused on the work. Welcome questions
and constructive disagreement. Critique behavior and code rather than attacking
people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse and deliberate disclosure of
another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Give credit for others' contributions and respect their licenses, confidentiality
and time. Do not share private customer projects or personal details as examples.

Ask a repository maintainer for a private channel to report a conduct concern;
do not publish sensitive details in an issue. If a report concerns a maintainer,
contact another maintainer where possible. Maintainers should handle reports with
care, limit sharing to those who need it, and avoid promising absolute secrecy.
They may moderate contributions or restrict participation to protect the community.

Before inviting public contributions, repository owners should designate the
maintainers handling these reports and make a private reporting channel available.
